About Silva Drip

Made for the block, not the boardroom.

Silva Drip started in a garage with one screen printer and a stack of blank hoodies. We still print everything in-house, in runs small enough that nothing sits in a warehouse. If a piece sells out, we move on to the next one instead of reprinting it.

No seasons, no lookbooks shot in a studio. Just drops, whenever the next idea is ready — designed on the block it's worn on, not in a boardroom.

Every graphic starts as a hand-drawn stamp before it ever touches fabric. That's why nothing in the store looks like it came off a template — because it didn't.
